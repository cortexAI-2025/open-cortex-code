Add a function wrapText(text, width) exported from src/wrap.js (ES module, no dependencies) that word-wraps text into lines of at most width characters and returns the lines joined by "\n". Rules: (1) width must be a positive integer, otherwise throw a RangeError; text must be a string, otherwise throw a TypeError. (2) Each paragraph (split on "\n"; "\r\n" counts as "\n") is wrapped independently, and blank paragraphs are kept as empty lines. (3) Inside a paragraph, runs of whitespace collapse to a single space and leading/trailing whitespace is removed. (4) Words are placed greedily: a word goes on the current line if it fits after one space separator, otherwise it starts a new line. (5) A word longer than width is split into chunks of exactly width characters, the last chunk stays on the current line and following words may continue after it. Write node:test tests in test/wrap.test.js. Do not modify existing files.
